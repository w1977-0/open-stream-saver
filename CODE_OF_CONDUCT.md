# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Open Stream Saver to be respectful, accessible, and constructive. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to communicate in good faith, welcome different experience levels, and focus discussion on the work rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

Use clear and respectful language. Give actionable feedback. Assume positive intent while asking clarifying questions. Credit prior work. Respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries. Keep public issues free of credentials, private media links, account data, and content that participants are not authorized to share.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, repeated disruptive behavior, sexualized language, and knowingly sharing private credentials or protected content are not acceptable. Requests to add DRM circumvention, credential theft, paywall bypassing, or other access-control bypasses are outside the project scope and may be closed without debate.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately by opening a GitHub security advisory when appropriate or by contacting the repository owner through GitHub. Reports should include only the details needed to understand the concern. Maintainers may request clarification, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
